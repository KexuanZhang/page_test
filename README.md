# Squish Lab

Squeeze, pull and pop five squishies on your phone: a cut watermelon, a red jelly, a bitten mochi, a stack of bubble-wrap sheets and a chocolate-crisp bun. Everything is simulated live (soft-body physics in the browser, WebGL2) and works with one or more fingers.

**Play:** https://kexuanzhang.github.io/page_test/

## Install on iPhone
1. Open the link above in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open *Squish Lab* from your home screen: it runs full screen, and after the first launch it also works offline.

(Android / Chrome: open the link, then menu → *Install app* or *Add to Home screen*.)

Tip: turn off silent mode to hear the sounds.
